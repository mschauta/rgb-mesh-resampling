# Contributors and AI assistance

This project was developed through sustained human–AI collaboration. The acknowledgements below are intended to make that collaboration explicit and to avoid presenting the repository as the unaided work of one person.

## Human direction and responsibility

- **[mschauta](https://github.com/mschauta)** — originated the research questions and experimental direction; made the modelling and design decisions; selected and interpreted the visual experiments; directed the iterative development; and takes responsibility for the claims and publication of the project.

## AI contributors

- **OpenAI Codex** — provided substantial assistance with mathematical derivations and verification, implementation and refactoring, test and benchmark design, result analysis, literature research, critical review, and technical editing.
- **Anthropic Claude** — provided substantial assistance with mathematical reasoning, method development, documentation, analysis, and editorial review.
- **Other AI-assisted tools** were used during exploration, checking, and drafting where appropriate.

The AI systems are credited for material contributions to the work, not merely for spelling or copy editing. They do not have independent GitHub identities, cannot appear as ordinary accounts in GitHub's automatically generated contributor graph, and do not bear authorship responsibility. Final decisions, verification, and responsibility remain with the repository owner.
